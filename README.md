# 🎓 AI Fundamentals for the Workplace — Capstone Portfolio

This repository contains the final capstone portfolio for the **L0-FAE — AI Fundamentals for the Workplace** training program.

The portfolio documents practical workplace-oriented applications of AI, with emphasis on clear prompting, professional communication, information processing, verification, responsible AI use, and integration of AI into everyday work tasks.

## 🎯 Capstone Purpose

The purpose of this capstone is to demonstrate the ability to use AI as a structured workplace assistant rather than simply generating unrestricted content. The work emphasizes defining the role, action, context, and expected output; reviewing AI-generated results; refining them based on requirements; and applying responsible-use principles before the final output is used.

## 🧩 Skills Demonstrated

- Prompt engineering and structured instructions.
- Professional workplace writing and rewriting.
- Summarization and information processing.
- Verification and fact-checking habits.
- Iterative refinement of AI-generated outputs.
- Safe and responsible use of AI tools.
- Protection of sensitive or confidential information.
- Practical integration of AI into daily workplace tasks.

## 🧠 Practical Approach

The capstone reflects a repeatable workplace AI workflow:

```text
Define the task
     ↓
Provide Role + Action + Context + Expected output
     ↓
Generate an initial AI response
     ↓
Review for accuracy, tone, privacy and relevance
     ↓
Refine the prompt / response
     ↓
Verify important information
     ↓
Use the final output responsibly
```

## 🔐 Responsible AI & Data Handling

The portfolio is designed around sanitized workplace examples. Sensitive information such as real employee identifiers, account numbers, confidential company information, customer data, passwords, or private organizational details should not be inserted into public AI prompts or public GitHub repositories.

AI-generated outputs should be reviewed by a human before workplace use, particularly when accuracy, policy compliance, privacy, or business decisions are involved.
